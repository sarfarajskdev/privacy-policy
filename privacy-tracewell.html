Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

1. Where This Policy Applies

This Privacy Policy applies to the Tracewell: One Line Puzzle mobile game ("Tracewell," "the App," "the Game"), available on the Apple App Store. It explains what information is collected when you play, why, and the choices you have. By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

2. Our Privacy Commitment

Tracewell is a calm, single-player puzzle game. Tracking for ads happens only if you allow it. You never create an account, and we never ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or precise location. Your game progress is saved on your device. The App is free to play and supported by ads; to understand how the game is used and to keep it stable, it also uses Google Firebase for anonymous analytics and crash reports. Everything is described below.

3. Information We Do NOT Collect

  • Your name, email address, phone number, or any account credentials (there are no accounts)
  • Your contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or calendar
  • Your precise GPS location
  • Your Apple ID or payment card details (purchases are handled entirely by Apple)
  • The Advertising Identifier (IDFA), unless you choose "Allow" when the App asks for tracking permission (see Section 8)

4. Game Data Stored on Your Device

To let you pick up where you left off, the App saves the following locally on your device only:

  • Progress: levels and chapters completed, seals earned, skipped levels, your in-progress attempt, hints remaining, Daily Figure history and streak, and play statistics (such as levels solved and time played)
  • Settings: sound, music, haptics, appearance and similar preferences
  • Ad limits: simple counters that cap how many ads you see per day

This data is not sent to our servers and is not synced to any cloud service by us. It is deleted when you delete the App.

5. Google Firebase Analytics

We use Google Firebase Analytics to understand how players experience the game, for example which levels are started, completed, or abandoned; where players get stuck; how often hints, skips, and settings are used; and which screens are opened. This helps us balance level difficulty and fix confusing parts of the game.

Along with these gameplay events, Firebase receives basic technical information: device model, iOS version, app version, language, an anonymous Firebase-generated app instance identifier, and approximate (country/region-level) location derived from your IP address.

  • Analytics data is not linked to your name or identity
  • The App uses the version of Firebase Analytics that does not access the Advertising Identifier (IDFA)
  • Analytics data is not used by us to show you ads and is not sold

6. Google Firebase Crashlytics

If the App crashes or encounters a serious error, Firebase Crashlytics sends a crash report so we can find and fix the problem. A crash report contains technical details such as the part of the code that failed, the device model, iOS version, app version, and an anonymous installation identifier. It does not contain personal information.

Google's handling of Firebase data is described at https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy/.

7. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Tracewell is free to play and shows ads provided by Google AdMob:

  • Banner ads at the bottom of some screens
  • Interstitial ads occasionally between levels (never during a puzzle and never in the first tutorial levels)
  • Rewarded video ads that you choose to watch in exchange for an extra hint or to skip a level. Watching them is always optional.

To deliver, measure, and limit ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect information from your device, such as IP address and approximate location, device and operating system information, app information, and how you interact with ads (views and taps). Google may also use cookies or similar technologies and device identifiers, such as an identifier for vendors. If you allow tracking when the App asks (see Section 8), Google may also use your device's Advertising Identifier (IDFA) to show more relevant ads and to measure them across apps and websites owned by other companies. If you do not allow tracking, the IDFA is not available to the App or to Google. Depending on your region and your choices, ads may be personalized or non-personalized.

Google's use of this information is governed by Google's Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy). You can learn how Google uses information from apps that use its services at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

8. Your Consent and Privacy Choices

  • App Tracking Transparency (all regions): the first time you open the App, iOS asks whether Tracewell may track your activity across other companies' apps and websites. If you choose "Allow," Google AdMob may use your Advertising Identifier (IDFA) for ads. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track," the IDFA is not shared, and the App and all of its features work exactly the same. You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • EEA, UK, and Switzerland: before any ads are requested, the App shows a consent message from Google's User Messaging Platform. You can accept, refuse, or manage the purposes for which your data is used. You can change your choice at any time in the App under Settings → Privacy choices.
  • United States (state privacy laws): where required, the App provides an option to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information for targeted advertising, also available under Settings → Privacy choices.
  • Limiting ad tracking on iOS: you can turn off personalized ads from Apple in Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising, and you can reset or limit identifiers through iOS settings.
  • Removing ads: you can remove ads by buying the in-app purchase described in Section 9, once it is available.

9. In-App Purchases (Remove Ads)

Tracewell may offer an optional one-time in-app purchase that removes ads. All purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store using Apple's StoreKit. We never receive your name, Apple ID, or payment card details. The App receives only a confirmation from Apple that the purchase is valid, so it can turn ads off and let you restore the purchase on your other devices. Apple's handling of payment information is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

10. Apple App Store Analytics

When you download Tracewell through the Apple App Store, Apple may collect information as part of its platform operations, governed by Apple's Privacy Policy, not ours. If you have opted into sharing App Analytics with developers in iOS Settings, Apple may share aggregated, anonymized usage and crash statistics with us. You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements.

11. Third-Party Services Summary

  • Google Firebase Analytics: anonymous gameplay analytics
  • Google Firebase Crashlytics: crash reports
  • Google AdMob and User Messaging Platform: ads and consent management
  • Apple StoreKit: in-app purchases

We do not sell your personal information for money, and we do not use data brokers.

12. What Happens If You Contact Us

If you contact us at feedback.sfsk@gmail.com for support or feedback, any information you provide is:

  • Used only to answer your request
  • Not added to any marketing list
  • Not shared with third parties
  • Deleted within 30 days after your issue is resolved

13. Data Retention and Deletion

On your device: game progress and settings remain on your device until you delete the App, which removes them.

Firebase: analytics data is kept by Google for a limited period (we use a retention period of no more than 14 months) and crash reports for up to 90 days, after which they are deleted automatically.

Advertising: data collected by Google for advertising is retained according to Google's policies.

Requests: because analytics and crash data are anonymous and not linked to your identity, we may not be able to locate a specific person's records. If you would like us to try, email us with the details in Section 17 and we will help where we can.

14. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, including under the GDPR (EU/UK), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA), and other laws, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict the use of your personal data, to object to its processing, to withdraw consent, and to opt out of targeted advertising. You can withdraw or change ad consent at any time in Settings → Privacy choices. For anything else, contact us using the details below. We will not discriminate against you for exercising any of these rights.

Where the GDPR applies, our legal bases are your consent (for personalized advertising and related storage on your device) and our legitimate interests (to keep the game working, measure its performance, fix crashes, and show non-personalized ads).

15. Children's Privacy

Tracewell is a puzzle game suitable for a general audience, but it is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us and we will take appropriate steps to delete it.

If a change materially reduces your privacy protections, we will notify you in the App before it takes effect. Continued use of the App after an update means you accept the revised policy.
